The Process

How we go from audit to automated growth.

A proven 4-stage operational framework that prevents wasted effort and keeps your brand moving forward:

1. Discovery & Audit

We review your existing social media profiles, Google Business Profile presence, past advertising data, and manual friction points to establish clear baseline metrics.

2. Strategy & 90-Day Calendar

I map out your 90-day content calendar, identify key promotional themes, draft audience targeting parameters in Meta Ads Manager, and design workflow automation blueprints.

3. Launch & Automate

Content is produced, reviewed, and scheduled in Meta Business Suite. Paid campaigns are launched, and custom n8n automations go live to handle lead alerts and routine syncs.

4. Track, Report & Optimise

Using Google Analytics and platform metrics, I provide clear bi-weekly reports detailing what performed best and where to allocate our focus in the next 90-day sprint.
